Application Category 01

Breakfast Foods & Flaked Cereals

Recommended: Grits 101 & 101A
🥣 Famous Breakfast Brands: Kellogg's Corn Flakes, Quaker Oats & Grits, General Mills, Post

Corn grits have been a cherished morning staple for centuries across the globe. When simmered gently with water, milk, or broth, coarse corn grits release natural starches that create a creamy, rich porridge with a toothsome, satisfying texture.

Breakfast Corn Flakes:

Large grits particles are pressure-cooked with malt syrup, dried, flaked between heavy chilled steel rollers, and toasted to crunchy perfection.

Hominy & Cheese Grits:

Served savory with sharp cheddar, butter, black pepper, and shrimp in Southern cuisine, or sweetened with honey and fresh fruit.

Instant Breakfast Cereals:

Fine cuts (Grits 112) cook in under 3 minutes, serving fast-paced urban households without losing natural corn fiber.

Gourmet Heritage 02

Italian Polenta & European Fine Dining

Recommended: Grits 101 & 108
🍝 European Culinary Icons: Italian Trattorias, Barilla, De Cecco, Winterfall Italian Finest

In Northern Italy and across Mediterranean gastronomy, coarse yellow corn grits (known as Polenta Bramata) are cooked into velvety, golden porridge and seasoned with truffles, wild mushrooms, gorgonzola, and sweet herbs.

Creamy Polenta Bowls:

Served steaming with braised short ribs, ratatouille, or sautéed wild garlic mushrooms.

Grilled & Fried Polenta Cakes:

Firm-chilled polenta cut into rustic triangles and seared over olive oil for crispy antipasti.

Gluten-Free Pasta & Gnocchi:

Micronized corn flour and meal blended into artisanal gluten-free pastas with firm al-dente bite.

Application Category 03

Bakery Products, Pizza Crusts & Fast Food

Recommended: Grits 108 & Corn Meal
🍕 Global Chains & Pizzerias: Domino's, Pizza Hut, BOX8, McDonald's, KFC, Subway

Commercial bakeries, pizzerias, and QSR fast-food chains prize corn grits for imparting authentic rustic appearance, crispy crust protection, and delightful textural crunch.

Pizza Dough Dusting (Domino's / Pizza Hut):

Corn grits under pizza dough act as friction-reducers for oven peels and create that blistered crunchy crust without scorching.

English Muffins (McDonald's):

Dusted beneath sourdough muffins to prevent griddle sticking while delivering that signature rough-textured golden crunch.

Extra-Crispy Coatings (KFC):

Blended into fried chicken dredges and fish batters to create stays-crispy crunch peaks under fast-food heat lamps.

Application Category 04

Extruded Snacks & Tortilla Nachos

Recommended: Grits 108 & Grits 112
🍿 Global Snack Titans: Cheetos, Doritos, Kurkure, Lay's, Takis, Funyuns

The global snack industry relies heavily on degermed corn grits. In twin-screw and single-screw snack extruders, high-pressure friction melts corn starches into a molten dough that instantly flashes into light, airy collets as it exits the die nozzle.

Cheese Puffs & Corn Curls:

Direct-expanded snacks require low fat (≤1%) and uniform mesh to expand evenly with an ultra-light, melt-in-the-mouth bite.

Crispy Tortilla Chips & Nachos:

Blended into masa for commercial tortilla chips, ensuring structural strength for heavy salsa dipping without breakage.

3D Pellets & Snack Rings:

Engineered into shelf-stable dry pellets shipped worldwide to fry-and-flavor manufacturers.

Application Category 04

Animal & Poultry Nutrition

Recommended: Calibrated Industrial Cuts

Maize is the gold standard energy grain in commercial animal agriculture. Our clean Maize Broken and coarse corn grits provide clean, high-carbohydrate energy free of dust, mycotoxins, and foreign debris.

Poultry Scratch & Broiler Feed:

Optimized particle size promotes healthy gizzard development and natural yolk pigmentation through natural carotenoids.

Dairy Cattle Rations:

Clean starch fractions offer controlled ruminal fermentation without inducing acidosis, boosting milk yield and butterfat content.

Aquaculture Pellets:

Serves as an essential carbohydrate binder in sinking and floating fish feed pellets.

Application Category 05

Industrial Brewing & Food Processing

Recommended: Grits 101A (Brewer's Low-Fat)

In modern industrial plants, corn grits serve as pure starch donors, textural modifiers, and clean fermentation adjuncts.

Lager Brewing Adjunct:

Grits 101A (≤0.8% fat) is cooked in cereal mash cookers alongside malted barley. Its neutral flavor produces light, dry, refreshing lagers with crystal clarity and white foam stability.

Soup Thickeners & Gravies:

Acts as a natural non-GMO thickening agent that withstands freeze-thaw cycles without water syneresis.

Binder in Meat Emulsions:

Maintains moisture retention and firm bite in processed sausages and vegan patties.

Classic Southern Cheddar Cheese Grits

🔥 Easy Prep 🧀 Rich & Creamy 🌾 Naturally Gluten-Free

Slow-simmered golden corn grits folded with rich aged sharp cheddar cheese, sweet cream farm butter, and a touch of black pepper. The undisputed king of Southern comfort cooking.

Key Ingredients:
  • 1 cup Coarse Corn Grits
  • 3 cups Veg / Chicken Broth
  • 1 cup Whole Milk
  • 1½ cups Sharp Cheddar
  • 3 tbsp Butter
  • Salt & Pepper
👨‍🍳 Quick Cooking Steps:
  1. Bring broth, milk, salt, and 1 tbsp butter to a rolling simmer in a heavy saucepan.
  2. Slowly whisk in corn grits in a steady stream to guarantee a lump-free texture.
  3. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally until thick.
  4. Remove from heat; fold in shredded cheddar and remaining butter until creamy. Serve hot!
⏱️ 30 Mins • 🍽️ 4 Servings
Recommended Cut: Grits 101 (Coarse)

Garlic Butter Shrimp & Grits

🦐 Lowcountry Classic 🥓 Smoky Bacon 🧄 Herb Garlic Pan Sauce

Juicy wild shrimp pan-seared with smoked paprika, crisp bacon, and minced garlic, ladled atop a warm, velvety bed of stone-milled coarse corn grits with fresh green scallions.

Key Ingredients:
  • 1 lb Fresh Jumbo Shrimp
  • 1 cup Coarse Grits 101
  • 4 slices Thick Bacon
  • 3 cloves Minced Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice
  • Sliced Scallions
👨‍🍳 Quick Cooking Steps:
  1. Cook coarse corn grits in salted water and milk until thick, buttery, and smooth. Keep warm.
  2. Fry chopped bacon until crispy in a skillet; remove bacon bits, reserving pan drippings.
  3. Toss seasoned shrimp in hot skillet for 2 minutes per side until pink; stir in garlic and lemon.
  4. Ladle warm grits into bowls, top with seared shrimp, spoon over garlic butter, and garnish with bacon.
⏱️ 20 Mins + Chill • 🍽️ 6 Servings
Recommended Cut: Grits 101 or 108

Crispy Golden Grit Cakes (Polenta Croquettes)

🌟 Ultra Crispy Exterior 🌿 Fresh Rosemary 🧀 Parmesan Crusted

Thick savory grits seasoned with parmesan cheese and cracked herbs, chilled into a block, cut into geometric cakes, and pan-seared to deep golden perfection. Crunchy outside, creamy inside!

Key Ingredients:
  • 1 cup Corn Grits
  • 3½ cups Seasoned Broth
  • ½ cup Grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp Chopped Rosemary
  • Olive Oil / Butter for frying
  • Flaky Sea Salt
👨‍🍳 Quick Cooking Steps:
  1. Cook grits thick; stir in parmesan cheese, fresh chopped rosemary, and black pepper.
  2. Spread into a buttered shallow square dish and chill in refrigerator for 45 minutes until firm.
  3. Slice the chilled block into triangles or squares using a sharp chef's knife.
  4. Pan-fry in olive oil over medium-high heat for 4 minutes per side until crackling crisp. Serve with dip!
⏱️ 15 Mins • 🍽️ 2 Servings
Recommended Cut: Grits 108 or 112

Wildflower Honey & Butter Breakfast Bowl

🍯 Sweet Morning Fuel 🫐 Fresh Berries 🥜 Crunchy Pecans

A wholesome country morning porridge. Medium corn grits cooked gently in milk and cinnamon, finished with a generous swirl of sweet farm butter, raw wildflower honey, toasted pecans, and berries.

Key Ingredients:
  • ¾ cup Corn Grits (Medium)
  • 2½ cups Milk (or Oat Milk)
  • 2 tbsp Sweet Butter
  • 2 tbsp Wildflower Honey
  • ¼ tsp Ground Cinnamon
  • Blueberries & Pecans
👨‍🍳 Quick Cooking Steps:
  1. Bring milk, cinnamon, and a tiny pinch of salt to a gentle simmer in a non-stick pot.
  2. Whisk in corn grits gradually; lower heat to low and simmer for 12–15 minutes until velvety.
  3. Stir in butter until melted and glistening throughout the hot porridge.
  4. Pour into warm bowls; swirl raw wildflower honey on top and garnish with berries and toasted pecans.

Master Chef Golden Rules for Perfect Corn Grits

Culinary Pro-Tips
1. The 4:1 Liquid Ratio

For coarse grits (Grits 101), always use 4 cups liquid (mix of broth and milk) per 1 cup grits. Grits need room to hydrate fully.

2. Continuous Initial Whisk

Never dump grits all at once. Pour in a slow steady trickle while whisking vigorously for the first 60 seconds to prevent starch clumps.

3. Low, Covered Simmer

Once simmering, turn heat to lowest setting and keep covered. Steam circulation allows corn endosperm to gelatinize without burning.

4. Finish With Fat Off Heat

Always take the pot off the flame before beating in cheese and cold butter. This emulsion creates a glossy, non-greasy velvet sheen.
