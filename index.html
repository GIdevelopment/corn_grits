About Corn Grits

Corn Grits are coarse, golden granules precision-milled from the fractured endosperm of choice yellow corn. Celebrated worldwide for their rich nutritional density, natural sweetness, and exceptional culinary versatility across commercial food processing, brewing, snacks, and wholesome kitchen recipes.
