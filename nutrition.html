Packaging & Industrial Storage Guidelines

Because dry corn grits have undergone mechanical degermination (removing 95%+ of the oil-rich germ), their shelf stability is substantially superior to whole grain maize. However, adhering to environmental protocols ensures peak gelatinization efficiency:

Moisture Management (≤ 13.5%)

Grits leave precision dry mills at 12.0% – 13.5% moisture. Warehouses must maintain relative humidity below 65% to prevent water uptake, fungal growth, or clumped feed lines.

Temperature & Sunlight

Store in a cool, well-ventilated dry warehouse below 25°C (77°F). Bags should be kept off concrete floors on clean wooden/plastic pallets away from direct solar UV radiation.

Shelf Life & Rotation

Unopened original bags possess a proven shelf life of 12 to 24 months. Implement strict FIFO (First-In, First-Out) inventory rotation to maximize production consistency.
