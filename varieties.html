🥣 Industrial Flaking Code: Grits 101
#10–#14 Sieve 1.4–2.0 mm Cut Flint Maize Endosperm

Grits 101 (Coarse Flaking)

Primary Breakfast Cereals & Heavy Porridge Cut

Grits 101 is the largest commercial granulation cut produced by precision dry-milling. Cut from the hard vitreous endosperm of cleaned flint corn with the germ completely excised, these large angular granules maintain structural integrity through aggressive steam-conditioning and mechanical roller flaking.

U.S. Mesh Range #10 – #14 Mesh
Metric Micron Size 1,400 – 2,000 μm
Crude Fat / Lipids ≤ 1.00% (Dry Basis)
Moisture Standard ≤ 13.50% (Max)
Bulk Density 0.72 – 0.82 g/cm³
Standard Shelf Life 12 Months Dry
⚙️ Compatible Machinery
  • Heavy-duty rotary steam cookers
  • Industrial twin-roll flaking mills
  • Slow-cook hominy porridge kettles
  • Low-shear agglomeration dies
🍽️ Food Products & Uses
  • Crispy breakfast golden corn flakes
  • Traditional Southern hominy grits bowls
  • Rustic artisan cornbread loaves
  • Granola & whole grain cluster crunch
🍺 Brewery Standard: Grits 101A
#12–#16 Sieve ≤0.8% Fat Ultra-Low Brewer's Extract ≥79%

Grits 101A (Brewer's Low-Fat)

Ultra-Low Lipid Fermentation Adjunct for Premium Beers

Engineered specifically for commercial brewing mash tuns. Through an advanced multi-stage degerming process, the lipid content is tightly calibrated under 0.8%, preventing oil haze, oxidation, or head retention collapse in pale lagers, American pilsners, and craft spirits.

U.S. Mesh Range #12 – #16 Mesh
Metric Micron Size 1,180 – 1,700 μm
Crude Fat / Lipids ≤ 0.80% (Ultra-Low)
Moisture Standard ≤ 13.00% (Strict)
Extract Yield (Dry) ≥ 79.5% Minimum
Standard Shelf Life 12 Months Dry
⚙️ Compatible Machinery
  • Commercial brewery cereal cookers
  • Automated mash tun agitators
  • Pneumatic grain transfer conveyors
  • Lauter tun mash separation systems
🍺 Beverage Products & Uses
  • Commercial crisp lager & pilsner beers
  • Clear malt beverages & hard seltzers
  • Bourbon & grain whiskey mash bills
  • Rapid starch conversion wort recipes
🍿 Snack Extrusion Benchmark: Grits 108
#14–#20 Sieve 0.85–1.4 mm Cut High Expansion 10–12x

Grits 108 (Medium Extrusion)

The Global Snack Benchmark for Direct-Expanded Puffs & Curls

The most versatile and highest-volume industrial corn grit in global food manufacturing. Calibrated within 850–1,400 microns, Grits 108 melts rapidly in high-shear extrusion barrels and expands instantaneously into uniform cellular puffs, curls, loops, and crunchies with zero blistering.

U.S. Mesh Range #14 – #20 Mesh
Metric Micron Size 850 – 1,400 μm
Crude Fat / Lipids ≤ 1.00% (Dry Basis)
Moisture Standard ≤ 13.50% (Max)
Volumetric Expansion 10x – 12x In Extruder
Standard Shelf Life 12 Months Dry
⚙️ Compatible Machinery
  • Single & twin-screw high-shear extruders
  • Continuous snack rotary baking ovens
  • Commercial seasoning tumbler drums
  • High-speed industrial pizza dough sheeters
🍿 Snack & Food Applications
  • Direct-expanded cheese balls & curls
  • Baked tortilla chips & nacho crisps
  • Commercial pizza crust bottom release
  • Artisan bakery cornbread & muffin premixes
🌾 Fine Granular Cut: Grits 112
#20–#30 Sieve 0.60–0.85 mm Cut Instant Hydration

Grits 112 (Fine Cut / Granular Meal)

Rapid-Cooking Fine Granulation for Crispy Batters & Baby Cereals

A finely calibrated granulation bridging the gap between medium grits and corn meal. Grits 112 hydrates quickly with hot or boiling water, creating silky smooth batters that hold superior crunch during deep frying or hot-air baking without absorbing excess cooking oil.

U.S. Mesh Range #20 – #30 Mesh
Metric Micron Size 600 – 850 μm
Crude Fat / Lipids ≤ 1.00% (Dry Basis)
Moisture Standard ≤ 13.50% (Max)
Hydration Time Fast ≤ 3 Minutes
Standard Shelf Life 12 Months Dry
⚙️ Compatible Machinery
  • High-shear wet batter slurry mixers
  • Industrial continuous deep fat fryers
  • Drum dryers for infant formula/cereal
  • Automated English muffin dusting units
🥟 Culinary & Industrial Uses
  • Crispy dredging for fried poultry & seafood
  • Infant hot porridge & instant weaning cereals
  • Commercial English muffin baking dusting
